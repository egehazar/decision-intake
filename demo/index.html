    Rules: PolicyEngine-US, 2026 rules, everyone assumed to take up what they're entitled to. Households: a resampled slice of PolicyEngine's Populace 2024, a calibrated synthetic population built from census and tax data, not real people. Each household's chance of eligibility comes from a model trained on PolicyEngine's results, scored on your answers where you've given them and on similar households' answers where you haven't. Amounts are estimates, not a determination of eligibility; a real screener must link to the official application.

    A question is worth asking when the mistakes it is expected to prevent outweigh the cost of asking, here a year per question.
