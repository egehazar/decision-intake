      How it works

      The DecisionIntake engine learned from 48,657 households scored by PolicyEngine-US, an open model of the 2026 benefit rules. After each answer it asks the question most likely to change what you should apply for, and stops when another question isn't worth your time.

      How well it does

      On 2,000 households it had never seen, it asked 8 questions on average instead of 18 and was as accurate as asking all 18. It beat the strongest decision tree and the best fixed 8-question form we could build. This is a screening estimate, not a decision: only the programme can tell you for sure.
